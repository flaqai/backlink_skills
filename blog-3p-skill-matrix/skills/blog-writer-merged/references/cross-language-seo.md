# Cross-language editorial method

Use this method inside W’s research-and-writing loop when the frozen campaign targets another language or region.

1. Inspect current public brand pages for the same locale and intent. Record URL, context and date. This is the highest-priority wording source.
2. When usable, use global Google Trends only with **English seed terms and English long-tail candidates**, regardless of the target language. Record the English source concept/query, `GT seed language = ENGLISH_ONLY`, scope, time window, date and relative signal. It is optional global relative-interest context only: do not compare target-language wording there or infer local search volume, low-base/high-momentum, local demand, commercial intent, or model capability.
3. If Trends is unavailable, insufficient, or inconclusive, do not force repeated collection or manufacture a trend rationale. Choose the long-tail reader problem from current brand wording and target-region SERP semantics. Record the evidence path and an explicit boundary that this is not a popularity or demand claim. Platform material may improve technical depth, example selection, tone or delivery, but can never establish a keyword, natural variant, search intent, local demand or popularity.
4. Inspect target-region SERPs to identify reader intent, natural phrasing and modifiers. Record query, date and semantic patterns; never copy competitors.
5. Select wording in strict order: `CURRENT_BRAND_SITE → REGIONAL_SERP → MODEL_TRANSLATION_FALLBACK`.
6. A fallback is permitted only after two independent regional-SERP checks document no usable consensus wording. Record both checks, source concept, proposed wording, language/intent rationale and cultural/legal risk. Never label it SERP-proven.

Freeze the long-tail rationale, `reader_intent_basis`, optional `platform_profile_use`, and English-concept-to-regional-SERP map in the canonical `research/evidence-pack.json`; `research/cross-language-seo.md` may be a derived reading view, never a second hand-maintained source. If `evidence_posture` is `METHOD_TEMPLATE_NO_EXECUTION`, frame comparisons as reader-run designs rather than results; documented empirical claims require a protocol, settings/inputs, run log, criteria and limitations. In the standard route, R independently judges reader-task fit, wording quality and evidence boundaries in its one final full review; an early research challenge exists only for the elevated route. Missing Trends alone is not a defect. G verifies only that the frozen localization requirement is represented in final delivery.
